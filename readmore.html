About the game

Nobu the main character in the game, once lived peacefully in the forest. But that all changed when an evil king took over the throne. The power made the king greedy and evil, he wanted everyone to obey him. The king is on his way to enslave and brainwash the whole forest and make everyone his slaves. The king is ruthless and is killing and destroying everything in his way, so be carefull. On your mission to stop the king and save the forest, you get captured by one of the kings brute.

In the demo you will only be able to see one area of the game: The one of the kings’ brutes have captured you and while waiting for the king to arrive to retake what he lost, the brute also wants to have some fun. The brute decides that to kill time you have to fight in different arenas in his colosseum. the colosseum is filled to the brim with enemies. You will need to fight through hordes and travel through weird terrain to be able to make it out of that horrible place with your life.
