About Us

We are two students from Sweden working on our final high school project. We have always been passionate about gaming and programming, and this interest inspired the idea for our game. Over the past year, we have been creating small games, and we decided it was time to take on a more challenging project.

All programming and most of the game design are done by us. But we have also received valuable support from our friends, who have helped with additional designs, music, and managing our social media.

This is our first time developing a larger scale game, and we approach it with no specific expectations. Our main goal is to learn, grow as developers, and most importantly, have fun throughout the process. We really hope you will enjoy playing the game as much as we enjoyed creating it.
